OpenVisio is a sovereign, open-source video conferencing solution hosted in Europe by Clever Cloud and built on [La Suite Meet](https://github.com/suitenumerique/meet).

Access happens directly in the browser, with no client to install, and audio and video flow over WebRTC. OpenVisio covers the expected use cases of a modern video conferencing tool, with built-in screen sharing, meeting recording, and automatic transcription.

Add OpenVisio as a location on your event types and Cal will automatically create a meeting room for every booking.
